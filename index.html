«4000 Go» — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 22-noyabr · Ishlab chiquvchi: Xolbekov Olimjon

Qisqacha:
  • ilova shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaydi, hech kimga yubormaydi va sotmaydi;
  • hisob (login) ochish shart emas, ilova internetsiz ishlaydi;
  • reklama va pullik xaridlar yo'q;
  • barcha natijalaringiz faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi.

Telefonda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Quyidagilar faqat sizning telefoningizda, ilovaning ichki xotirasida saqlanadi. Ishlab chiquvchi ularni ko'rmaydi va olmaydi:

  • ism — faqat o'zingiz kiritsangiz (ixtiyoriy). U ilovada sizga murojaat qilish va sertifikatga yozish uchun ishlatiladi;
  • profil rasmi — faqat o'zingiz tanlasangiz (ixtiyoriy);
  • o'rganish natijalari: so'zlar reytingi, o'qilgan hikoyalar, test natijalari, olmoslar, unvonlar, sertifikatlar, kunlik faollik (seriya);
  • ilovada o'tkazilgan vaqt — faqat statistika ekrani uchun;
  • hikoyalarga qo'ygan baholaringiz;
  • sozlamalar: daraja, ko'rinish (kunduzgi/tungi), ovoz, matn o'lchami, eslatma vaqti.

Ilova familiya, tug'ilgan sana, jins, telefon raqami, manzil yoki joylashuvingizni so'ramaydi.

Zaxira nusxa

Agar telefoningizda Android'ning zaxira nusxa xizmati yoqilgan bo'lsa, ilova sozlamalari va natijalari boshqa ilovalar ma'lumotlari kabi sizning Google hisobingizga avtomatik zaxiralanishi mumkin. Bu yangi telefonda natijalarni tiklashga yordam beradi. Zaxirani Android va Google boshqaradi, ishlab chiquvchi unga kira olmaydi. Uni telefon sozlamalarida o'chirib qo'yishingiz mumkin.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Ilovani o'chirsangiz, barcha ma'lumotlar o'chadi. Ilovani o'chirmasdan tozalash uchun: Sozlamalar → Ilovalar → 4000 Go → Xotira → Ma'lumotlarni tozalash.

Ruxsatlar

  • Bildirishnomalar — kunlik eslatma va yopilgan savollar qayta ochilgani haqida xabar berish uchun. Rad etishingiz yoki keyinroq o'chirishingiz mumkin.

Ilova kamera, mikrofon, joylashuv, kontaktlar va fayllaringizga kirmaydi. Profil rasmi Android'ning rasm tanlash oynasi orqali olinadi: ilova faqat siz tanlagan bitta rasmni ko'radi.

Google Play xizmatlari

  • Yangilanishlar. Yangi versiya bor-yo'qligini telefoningizdagi Google Play ilovasi tekshiradi (Google Play In-App Updates).
  • Baho qoldirish. Vaqti-vaqti bilan ilova Google Play'ning baho oynasini ko'rsatishi mumkin (Google Play In-App Review). Baho va sharhni Google Play qabul qiladi va ularga Google'ning o'z qoidalari amal qiladi. Ilova qanday baho qo'yganingizni bilmaydi.

Ikkala holatda ham ishlab chiquvchi sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni olmaydi.

Talaffuz (ovoz)

Inglizcha so'zlar telefoningizning o'z «matnni nutqqa aylantirish» (TTS) xizmati orqali o'qiladi. Ilova bu xizmatga faqat o'qiladigan inglizcha so'z yoki gapni beradi.

Ulashish va fikr bildirish

  • Ulashish. «Ulashish» tugmasini bossangiz, ilova natijangiz yoki sertifikatingiz tasvirini yaratadi va telefoningizning ulashish oynasini ochadi. Tasvir faqat siz tanlagan ilovaga va faqat siz yuborsangiz ketadi. Sertifikatda o'zingiz kiritgan ism bo'lishi mumkin.
  • Taklif va mulohazalar. «Telegram orqali yuborish» tugmasi Telegram'ni muallif bilan suhbatda tayyor xabar bilan ochadi. Xabarda faqat kitob, unit yoki hikoya raqami, bahoyingiz va o'zingiz yozgan matn bo'ladi. Xabarni o'zingiz yuborasiz, ilovaning o'zi hech narsa yubormaydi.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalar uchun ham mo'ljallangan va Google Play «Families» talablariga amal qiladi. Biz 13 yoshdan kichik bolalardan ham, boshqa foydalanuvchilardan ham shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz. Ilovada reklama, pullik xarid, chat yoki foydalanuvchilar o'rtasida muloqot yo'q.

O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, yangi matn shu sahifada e'lon qilinadi va yuqoridagi sana yangilanadi.

Privacy Policy — “4000 Go”

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Developer: Xolbekov Olimjon

In short:
  • the app does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal data;
  • no account is needed, and the app works offline;
  • there are no ads and no in-app purchases;
  • all your progress is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see or receive it:

  • your name, only if you choose to enter it (optional). It is used to greet you and to print on certificates;
  • a profile photo, only if you choose one (optional);
  • learning progress: word ratings, stories read, test results, diamonds, titles, certificates and your daily streak;
  • time spent in the app, used only for the statistics screen;
  • your story ratings;
  • settings: level, light or dark theme, voice, text size and reminder time.

The app does not ask for your surname, date of birth, gender, phone number, address or location.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on, the app's settings and progress may be backed up automatically to your Google account, like other apps' data, so you can restore them on a new phone. Android and Google manage this backup; the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. To clear it without uninstalling: Settings → Apps → 4000 Go → Storage → Clear data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — for the optional daily reminder and for telling you when locked questions open again. You can refuse or turn them off at any time.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. A profile photo is chosen through Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the single photo you select.

Google Play services

  • Updates. The Google Play app on your device checks for new versions (Google Play In-App Updates).
  • Ratings. From time to time the app may show Google Play's rating dialog (Google Play In-App Review). Ratings and reviews are received by Google Play under Google's own terms; the app does not know what rating you give.

In both cases, the developer receives no personal data about you.

Pronunciation

English words are spoken by your device's built-in text-to-speech service. The app only passes it the English word or sentence to be read aloud.

Sharing and feedback

  • Sharing. When you tap “Share”, the app creates an image of your result or certificate and opens your device's share sheet. The image goes only to the app you choose, and only if you send it. A certificate may show the name you entered.
  • Feedback. “Send via Telegram” opens Telegram with a prepared message to the author. The message contains only the book, unit or story number, your rating and the text you wrote. You send it yourself; the app sends nothing on its own.

Children

The app is designed for children as well as adults and follows the Google Play Families policy. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 or from anyone else. There are no ads, purchases, chats or contact between users.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
